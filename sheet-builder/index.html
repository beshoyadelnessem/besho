How this follows your sheets
  1. Pick the area and side, the way each sheet starts (Cervical, Dorsolumbar, (R) knee…).
  2. Examination: tenderness, ROM, weakness, tests, edema, control and proprioception.
  3. Medical treatment in your usual order: current, then cold, then laser, then extras.
  4. Rehabilitation in your progression, area by area, ending with home exercises.
  5. Precautions go in brackets after the program, like “[No resisted exs or manual exs]”.
  6. Next visit, “Repeat last session” brings the same program back, or writes “As previous”.

Options show the sheet numbers they were read from (S4 = sheet 4). Nothing here diagnoses: you choose, the page only writes it out.
